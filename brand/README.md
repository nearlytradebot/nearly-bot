## The mark

A chat bubble holding a command prompt, `>_`: you launch a token by sending a message.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal lime | `#C8F545` | The mark, highlights, primary buttons |
| Lime top / bottom | `#DBFF6B` → `#B8E62E` | The mark's gradient |
| Ink | `#141700` | Outline and prompt on the mark, text on lime |
| Night | `#0A0B08` | Dark backgrounds |
| Paper | `#F6F5F0` | Light backgrounds |

## Type

- **Bricolage Grotesque** ExtraBold for the wordmark "nearly" and headings.
- **Geist Mono** SemiBold for the "BOT" tag and code.
- **Geist** for body text.

All three are free on Google Fonts.

## Please

- Keep the mark's proportions and colors. Don't rotate, recolor, stretch or add effects to it.
- Leave clear space around it of at least a quarter of its width.
- Use the dark banner on dark backgrounds and the light banner on light ones.
- Use the assets to refer to Nearly Bot, not as your own brand or to suggest an endorsement. See
  [LICENSE](../LICENSE).
