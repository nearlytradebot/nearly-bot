# FAQ

**Can I launch on the website?**
No. Launch from the [Telegram bot](https://t.me/nearlytradebot) or with a post to
[@nearlytradesbot](https://x.com/nearlytradesbot) on X. The website is for swapping, bridging and following
launches.

**What does a launch cost?**
About 0.16 NEAR of storage plus the 0.05 NEAR fee and gas, so keep about 0.3 NEAR in your wallet. A large logo
or a tax adds a little more storage. The bot shows the exact total before you confirm. See [fees.md](fees.md).

**Who holds my keys?**
For bot wallets, Nearly Bot does, encrypted. For wallets you connect on the website, you do. See
[wallets-and-security.md](wallets-and-security.md).

**Can liquidity be pulled?**
No. The whole supply goes into the token's Rhea pool, and the pool belongs to the nearly.trade factory.

**How do creators earn?**
0.64% of every trade in the token's pool, paid out automatically about hourly. An optional tax (set at launch)
can add more.

**Why doesn't @nearlytradesbot tweet every launch?**
It announces launches made on X. Every launch, from Telegram and X, is posted in the
[launches channel](https://t.me/nearlybotlaunches) and listed on the board.

**Can I use the same wallet on Telegram and X?**
No. Each is separate: several wallets per Telegram user, one per X account. On Telegram you can `/import` a
wallet you already own.

**Where do the fees go?**
Buybacks, burns and platform development. See [fees.md](fees.md).

**Is Nearly Bot audited?**
Not yet. See the risk notice in the [README](../README.md#risk-notice).
