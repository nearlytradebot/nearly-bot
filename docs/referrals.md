# Invite & earn (Telegram)

Invite friends to the Telegram bot and earn from every token they launch there.

1. In [@nearlytradebot](https://t.me/nearlytradebot), send `/invite` (or tap **🎁 Invite & earn** on Home).
2. Share your link: `https://t.me/nearlytradebot?start=ref_…`.
3. When someone starts the bot through your link and accepts the terms, they're tied to you for good. You get a
   message.
4. Every token they launch **from the Telegram bot** pays you **0.01 NEAR** of the 0.05 NEAR launch fee. It's
   sent straight to your active wallet, and you get a message each time.

Your friend pays the same 0.05 NEAR either way; the fee is just split.

## Rules

- Telegram bot launches only. Launches made on X don't pay referrals.
- One inviter per user, set the first time they start the bot. You can't refer yourself.
- Payouts go to whichever of your wallets is active at the time of each launch.
- `/invite` shows how many people you've invited, their paid launches and your total earnings.
